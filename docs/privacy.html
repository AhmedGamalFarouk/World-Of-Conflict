World Of Conflict: privacy policy

World Of Conflict is an offline Android game. It makes no network requests and collects no data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party services.

What stays on your phone

The app keeps these on the device only, and uninstalling removes them:

  • your settings (for example vibration and graphics options),
  • a count of games started and finished, and their lengths,
  • one saved game, so you can continue it later,
  • a short error log. It is only shared if you choose Copy error log in About and paste it into a bug report yourself.

Permissions

The app declares the INTERNET permission only because the Android web view requires it; it makes no requests. It also uses VIBRATE for haptic feedback, which you can switch off in settings.
